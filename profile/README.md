## About us

Engineer & Sputnik Productions is an independent
engineering and creative studio exploring the intersection
of human creativity, technology and artificial intelligence.

We turn ideas into real things.

### Our fields of exploration

- Embedded systems and operating platforms
- Software architecture and engineering tools
- Robotics and automation
- Artificial intelligence and human-machine collaboration
- Music, art and creative technologies

### Our philosophy

Technology is not the destination.
It is a means of creation.

We believe in curiosity, craftsmanship, simplicity
and the freedom to invent.
